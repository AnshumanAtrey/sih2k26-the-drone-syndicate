How do you know the algorithms are right?

Because they are checked against brute force. The route planner is compared to exhaustive permutation search and the knapsack to exhaustive subset enumeration; the fusion rule is asserted to collapse to classical Chair-Varshney when the correlation matrix is the identity.

What each one does, and why that data structure

AlgorithmStructureComplexityWhat it buys
Two-pass descent
SAHI, arXiv 2202.06934		 greedy min-window-cover · spatial hash · union-find · weighted box fusion O(n log n) plan
O(n α(n)) merge		 A prone adult is 22 px at 30 m and 45 px at 15 m. Descending is resolution, not opinion. Covering six clustered candidates takes one crop, not six — every crop is a forward pass we pay for in joules.
Correlated fusion
generalises Chair-Varshney 1986		 sparse Map on packed int keys · Gauss-Jordan solveO(1) per update
O(k³), k≤8		 Thermal and RGB both die behind a slab, so summing them double-counts one physical vote. Inverse-covariance weights discount redundancy. At r=0.9 the pair is worth barely more than one independent stream.
Route planning
prize-collecting Held-Karp		 bitmask DP over subsetsO(2k·k²)
k ≤ 15		 Greedy next-best-view strands the aircraft far from base. This is the exact optimum with return-to-base as a hard constraint inside the DP, not a check bolted on after.
Link budget
0/1 knapsack		 1-D rolling DP + choice tableO(n·B) LoRa carries a few hundred bytes. A bare geo-record is 300 B, a thumbnail 8.3 kB. Sending in arrival order wastes the frame; this packs it optimally.

The arithmetic that matters

A 30 m sweep at 4 m/s with 50% frame overlap needs a new frame every 4.55 s — 0.22 Hz. The board delivers 539 FPS. That headroom is not for one fast detector: it is for running detection, visual-inertial odometry, an open-vocabulary encoder and the radio scanner at once — Qualcomm's own "run more networks simultaneously" claim, with a workload attached.

Detector: YOLOv8n, COCO weights, 640×640 — a fielded build fine-tunes on aerial search-and-rescue imagery. The algorithms above are the contribution; the weights are stock and we say so. Example images are our own renders; upload a real photograph to test it properly. Thermal and BLE streams are slider-driven — the RGB and descent streams are computed from your image.
