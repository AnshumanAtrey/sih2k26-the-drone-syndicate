## Why there is no image here

The template prescribes six fixed fields on this page. A full-bleed hero behind them costs contrast,
costs file size, and buys nothing — the judge has not yet been given a reason to care about a
beautiful drone. The reason is on Slide 2. Keep this page quiet, typographic and fast to read, and
spend the visual budget on the four content pages that are actually scored.

Ground: flat `#0B1220`. One 2 px cyan rule under the wordmark. Nothing else.

## Naming note

The idea title leads with the *outcome* ("sweeps a disaster zone in hours, not days"), not the
architecture. "LLM-commanded swarm" was the old title; it described our cleverness rather than the
judge's problem, and "LLM" invites a scepticism we do not need to absorb on page one. The language
model is a genuine part of the system and it is explained on Slide 3, where we have room to justify it.
